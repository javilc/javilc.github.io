The documents for your route

We do not have your diagnosis yet, so this is the most common list. You can mark any document that does not apply to you.

  • Passport and NIEIdentifies you on Modelo 149 and every later filing.
  • Proof of tax residence abroad for the last 5 yearsYou must not have been a Spanish tax resident in the five tax years before the move, and the burden of proof is yours.
  • Social Security registration (alta) showing the dateIt is the date the 6-month clock starts from. AEAT checks it against Social Security’s own records.
  • Employment contractIt evidences the cause of the move: employment ordered or accepted before you became resident.
  • Evidence of remote work for the foreign employerAEAT needs to see that the work is done remotely for a foreign employer, not for a Spanish client or branch.
  • Certificate of coverage (A1 or equivalent) showing the dateWhen you keep your home-country social security, this certificate replaces the Spanish registration as the document that starts the clock.
  • Posting letter from your employer, dated before the moveFor a relocation ordered by your employer, this letter proves the company sent you rather than you moving on your own.
  • Company documents: incorporation, ownership, your roleWhen the employer is your own company or you are joining a board, AEAT checks who owns it, what it does and your role in it.
  • Deed or document evidencing your appointment as directorFor the director route this is the document that evidences the cause of the move.

Signing in to the tax agency

Do you have a digital certificate or Cl@ve to file at the AEAT electronic office?

How to get one

  • Cl@ve: register with the invitation letter the tax agency posts to your address in Spain, or by video call from the Cl@ve website.
  • FNMT digital certificate: request it on the FNMT website, prove your identity at a registration office, and download it on the same computer.
  • Start now: it can take several days, and you need it before you can file.

We will never ask for your digital certificate or your Cl@ve password.

Registration number of your documentation

  1. Before Modelo 149, the documents go to the tax agency through its procedure “Aportar documentación necesaria para optar por el régimen especial”, on the AEAT website.
  2. You sign in with a digital certificate or Cl@ve and attach the documents from step 1.
  3. The receipt of that submission shows a registration number: that number goes in box 32.

      Points that need professional judgement

      • —You are paid by a company you own or control. The tax agency may not accept the employment relationship when the employee controls the employer, and this case is never eligible for Guided filing.
      • —You are a director: the form distinguishes an ordinary entity from an asset-holding one, and a stake of 25% or more changes the answer.
      • —The start-up or entrepreneurial routes depend on a favourable report or on the company’s registration, which the tax agency checks.
      • —Your route does not match any of the causes the form lists. A professional has to look at the case before anything is filed.
