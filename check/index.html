    Question 1/10
    Have you been a tax resident in Spain in any of the last 5 calendar years?

    Tax resident usually means more than 183 days in Spain in a calendar year, or your main economic interests here. Short stays and tourism do not count.

    Have you ever been in the Beckham regime before?

    Someone who renounced the regime, or was excluded from it, cannot opt in again. Completing the six years and moving abroad again is a different situation.

    What brings you to Spain?

    Your citizenship and permit situation

    Your citizenship and work permit

    A non-EU citizen needs a residence and work permit for the job itself; without it the employment relationship cannot start, and the clock does not start either.

    Is that employer a company you own or control?

    A significant stake, or a director role, means the employment relationship has to fit precisely the criteria the tax agency applies. The same goes for an employer with its own presence in Spain.

    Are you a shareholder or a director of that Spanish company?

    Owning or controlling your employer means the employment relationship has to fit precisely the criteria the tax agency applies. A minority stake or a seat on the board is reviewed before filing.

    Who are you being posted to in Spain?

    A posting to a company of the group, or to your own company, needs a more precise fit than one to a client or a project: what matters is who directs your work and who bears the salary.

    The company you will be a director of

    An ordinary company and an asset-holding (patrimonial) one are different boxes on Modelo 149, and with an asset-holding company a stake of 25% or more takes the route away.

    Your stake in it

    Do you have the favourable ENISA report?

    The entrepreneurial route depends on a favourable report classifying the activity as entrepreneurial and of economic interest for Spain.

    What kind of work is it?

    This route is for highly-qualified professionals serving start-ups, or for training, research, development and innovation.

    Will more than 40% of your total business, professional and employment income come from that activity?

    Tell us in one line what brings you to Spain

    The qualifying cause has to be identified before anything is filed; this line is what a professional starts from.

    Where will you pay social security contributions?

    With an A1 (EU/EEA/UK) or a certificate of coverage under a totalization agreement (for example US–Spain, up to 5 years) you keep contributing in your home country and pay nothing to Spanish Social Security. The certificate’s date is then what starts your 6-month clock.

    Where will you pay social security contributions?

    Without an employment contract you register yourself: as self-employed (RETA) or, as a director, under the regime that fits your role. With an A1 or a certificate of coverage you keep contributing in your home country, and the certificate’s date is what starts your 6-month clock.

    Your registration date

    The 6-month window for Modelo 149 starts with your Social Security registration in Spain, or with the date on your certificate of coverage if you keep your home-country social security. Not your arrival, not your contract signature.
