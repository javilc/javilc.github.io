9. Guided filing, Annual filing and the Relocation tax binder: terms of service

Provider, payment and prices. Guided filing (Presentación guiada), Annual filing (Declaración anual, Modelo 151) and the Relocation tax binder (Dossier fiscal de traslado) are automated self-service products provided by Elite Digital Services LLC, the owner of this website identified above, under the trade name Beckham Law Agency, which answers for them under these conditions. The payment is processed by Stripe as authorised reseller (merchant of record): Stripe is the seller towards you for the purchase, handles the transaction and takes care of the tax on the sale, and the service itself is provided by Elite Digital Services LLC. Prices are in US dollars: the price shown is the total price of the service and the one charged at the till. The purchase is made on the Site through Stripe's checkout, after the free diagnosis: Guided filing is sold only to a case the diagnosis says fits, the diagnosis goes with the purchase, and access to the service (the online case file and the walkthrough) opens by itself as soon as the payment is confirmed, with no form to fill in and nobody to wait for.

Guided filing: scope. A self-service information and support product so that you file Modelo 149 yourself. It is delivered as soon as it is bought and it runs automatically, with no review by a person at any point, and it uses an artificial-intelligence model to read the documents you upload and to produce the checks and the texts it gives you; your deadline and the mandatory boxes of the form are worked out by the provider’s own code, never by that model. It includes: a diagnosis of your route and of the requirements the regime asks of you; your Modelo 149 deadline, calculated from your registration date, with automatic notices before it runs out; the document checklist for your route; an online case file with automatic reading of what you upload; a step-by-step Modelo 149 walkthrough with your own data, for filing with Cl@ve or a digital certificate; an automatic check that no mandatory box of the form is missing before you file; a PDF of your case file; and automatic reminders for the annual Modelo 151 plus a notice in year 5 about leaving the regime (these yearly reminders are part of the paid products and are not sent to anyone who has not bought one). Price: $189, total price, one-off payment. Only for cases that meet all the entry criteria published on the Guided filing page.

Annual filing: scope. The equivalent product for Modelo 151, for people already in the regime with a single salary and, at most, bank interest: data collection through a form, an automatic check of the data the form asks for, a step-by-step Modelo 151 walkthrough for the user to file with their own credentials, and an automatic reminder for the following year. It is delivered immediately and automated on the same terms as Guided filing. Price: $149 per year, total price. Any other income (rentals, dividends, capital gains, more than one payer) is outside its scope.

Relocation tax binder: scope. A PDF document generated automatically by the provider’s own code from the answers you gave in the free diagnosis, with no review by a person and without any artificial-intelligence model: a letter to your employer asking for the documents your route needs, in English and Spanish; a timeline with your Modelo 149 deadline, calculated from the registration date you gave, and the tax years of the regime with their Modelo 151; and the document checklist for your route, with where to get each document. It is emailed to you as soon as the payment is confirmed, or as soon as you complete the diagnosis if you bought it without one. Every date in it depends on the date you gave: if that date is wrong, so are the dates. It is offered only with a diagnosis that shows no exclusion; if, when it is generated, your diagnosis shows one, no binder is generated and the price is refunded in full. Price: $29, total price, one-off payment. It is informational: it does not assess your case, it is not tax or legal advice and it does not include anything listed under Guided filing.

Exclusions (all three). The provider does not file on your behalf, does not represent you before the Spanish Tax Agency and does not give tax or legal advice. The filing is made by you with your own credentials, and responsibility for its content is yours. Not included: any review by a person of your diagnosis or of your data, and any written confirmation that your case fits; guidance on answering a requirement from the Spanish Tax Agency; US tax obligations (IRS, FEIE, Foreign Tax Credit, FBAR, FATCA); the filings of family members; answering tax-agency requirements on your behalf; and cases outside the published criteria, which are referred to a registered lawyer or tax advisor.

Immediate delivery, no response time. The three products are delivered as soon as they are bought, so there is no response time to state and no initial review to wait for: nothing in them depends on us answering. Whether a case meets the entry criteria is decided by the free diagnosis, before you buy, and Guided filing is only offered for the cases that meet them. Immediate delivery does not by itself touch your right of withdrawal: what happens to that right is set out below.

Fit guarantee (Guided filing and Annual filing). The free diagnosis decides, before the purchase, whether a case is a fit for the product. If after buying it the product finds that the case needs a registered professional, the provider undertakes, where you write to the contact email above within 30 days of the purchase, either to refund the price in full or to put it towards the fee of the professional who takes the case, whichever you prefer. This guarantee is given by the provider in addition to your statutory rights and does not limit them.

Pilot and written exceptions. During the launch phase the provider may offer Guided filing free of charge to a limited number of clients, under conditions sent to each of them in writing (by email) before any work starts; anything those conditions do not cover is governed by this section. A client taken on before 3 October 2026 keeps what was promised to them in writing at the time, including the written review of their diagnosis and of their data, even though it is no longer part of these products. An exception to the entry criteria is valid only when it is stated in writing in those conditions, together with what stays outside the service because of it. The user provides complete and truthful data and documents and tells the provider of any change in their situation; the walkthrough, screenshots and templates delivered with the service are for the user’s personal use only. A free engagement is not invoiced, creates no obligation to contract anything later, and the provider may end it, in writing, if the case stops meeting the criteria or the user does not provide the data and documents it needs.

The purchase itself is sold by Stripe as authorised reseller (merchant of record), so Stripe’s own terms and its own handling of refunds apply to it alongside this section.
