How the 6 months are counted

  • ▸ Six months date-to-date from the trigger date: a registration on 10 March gives 10 September as the last day. If the month has no such day, the last day of that month applies.
  • ▸ The clock starts with the Social Security registration or the certificate of coverage, not with arrival, the contract, the NIE or the visa.
  • ▸ The deadline is preclusive: a late Modelo 149 is rejected without assessing the rest of the case.
  • ▸ Spouse and children under 25 each have their own 6-month window, counted from their own trigger date.
