1. Overview

This policy explains how the Platform (Beckham Law Agency) processes personal data collected through this Site, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and Digital Rights (LOPDGDD). It covers the Site and the matching service only. Once a professional accepts your case, the data you exchange with them is processed by that professional as an independent controller, under their own privacy policy, engagement terms and professional-secrecy obligations.

2. Data we process

We only process the personal data you actively provide:

  • Call requests for Guided filing or Annual filing: the service you select, name, email address, your phone number if you give it, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Case records: when you send a form, we keep a record of your request, the consent you gave (the wording, date and page), whether you asked for follow-up emails and reminders, the registration date you entered in the diagnosis, and the emails we have sent you.
  • Matching requests: name, email address, the service you are interested in, your work situation, your Social Security registration status, the country you are moving from and the free-text description of your case that you choose to write. We ask you not to include identity documents, payslips or contracts.
  • Booking data: name, email address, time zone and any details you include when booking a call through the embedded Cal.com scheduler.
  • Lead forms: your email address and the estimate or answer details you actively choose to send us, for example when requesting your calculator estimate by email or asking for one of our guides.
  • Case file (“Prepare your case”): the details Modelo 149 asks for — tax identification number, name and surnames, phone number, address for notifications, dates of entry and start of activity, last country of residence, nationality, the cause of your move and the identification of your employer or entity — the name, tax identification number, date of birth and date of entry of each family member opting in with you, the registration number of the documentation you filed beforehand, and the documents you upload for your route (identity, employment or posting documents, Social Security registration or certificate of coverage).
  • Correspondence: personal data contained in emails you send to the contact address shown on the Site.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log IP addresses and basic request metadata for security and operational purposes.
  • Advertising measurement data (only if you accept cookies): when you arrive from one of our Google Ads ads and then send a form or tap the WhatsApp button, Google Ads records that a conversion happened, using its own cookies and identifiers (such as the ad click ID), your IP address and browser data. We do not send Google your name, your email or the contents of any form.

3. What stays in your browser

The free diagnosis and the savings calculator run entirely in your browser. The answers and figures you enter are not transmitted to our servers and are not stored by us, unless you subsequently choose to send them to us, for example by booking a consultation, requesting your estimate by email, or writing to us.

The only exception is anonymous usage statistics (see the cookies section below): we record that the calculator was used and which step and outcome category the diagnosis reached (for example “eligible” or “outside the deadline”), never your individual answers, your salary figure, your email or any identifier that could link those statistics to you.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data for the following purposes, on the following legal bases (art. 6 GDPR):

  • Managing call bookings and responding to enquiries, steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Putting you in contact with an independent professional who can take your case, which requires sharing your matching request with that professional, steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Complying with legal obligations, tax, accounting and, where applicable, anti-money-laundering rules (art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Keeping the Site secure: our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Sending you information you have expressly requested, your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Measuring the effectiveness of our advertising (Google Ads conversion tracking), your consent given in the cookie banner (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 22.2 LSSI-CE), which you may withdraw at any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.
  • Sending you the acknowledgement of each form and the email copy of your diagnosis or estimate, which you ask for when you send the form (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Sending you the reminders related to your case, which is what you agree to in the privacy box of each form: a notice before your Modelo 149 deadline, two short follow-up emails (on days 3 and 7) if you asked us for a service — Guided filing, Annual filing or a professional — and never for a diagnosis or an estimate on its own, and, once you have a registration date, a reminder every April while the regime lasts that the annual Modelo 151 is due, plus a notice in the fifth year. Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 21 LSSI-CE), which you may withdraw at any time. Every one of those emails carries a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops all of them, the yearly ones included.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for: matching requests, enquiries and booking data for 12 months after the last contact, so that we can follow up on the outcome of the matching, and thereafter only as required by statute. The professional who takes your case keeps their own engagement file under the retention periods that apply to them. Data processed on the basis of consent is kept until you withdraw it. If you ask for yearly reminders, we keep your email address, name and registration date for as long as the reminders last, and at most until the end of the regime period (seven years from that date); unsubscribing stops every email immediately. The documents you upload to a case file are deleted automatically 90 days after the case file is created if you do not engage a service, together with the case-file PDF generated from them; if you do engage one, they are kept for as long as that service and its legal obligations require. The rest of the case file (the Modelo 149 details) follows the 12-month rule above. You can ask us to delete your documents earlier at any time.

6. Recipients and processors

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Data is disclosed only to: (i) the independent professional assigned to your matching request, who receives the information you submitted and processes it as an independent controller, bound by professional secrecy — and, only when you ask us to pass your case on, read-only access to your case file and its documents through a link that expires and that we can revoke; (ii) service providers acting as processors under art. 28 GDPR, the scheduling provider (Cal.com), the form service that receives our web forms (Formspree), Cloudflare (hosting of our case system, its database and the storage of your documents: Cloudflare Workers, Workers KV and R2), Resend (sending of acknowledgements, follow-ups and reminders), and our hosting and email providers; (iii) Google Ireland Limited (Google Ads), only if you accept advertising cookies, which receives the conversion measurement data described in section 2 and processes it under its own advertising terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy); and (iv) public authorities and courts where legally required.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, Cal.com, Formspree, Google, Cloudflare and Resend in the United States). Where that happens, transfers rely on a European Commission adequacy decision or on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by it, together with additional safeguards where needed.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the LOPDGDD you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”) where there is no legal ground to retain it.
  • Restrict processing in the cases foreseen by art. 18 GDPR.
  • Receive your data in a portable format (art. 20 GDPR).
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

9. How to exercise your rights

Write to the privacy contact address above, attaching proof of identity; we will reply within one month (art. 12 GDPR). If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD, www.aepd.es).

10. Cookies

Cloudflare Web Analytics and Umami measure our audience without cookies: they store no identifier on your device and do not track you across websites; they record aggregated, anonymous data such as pages viewed, referring site, country, device type and which buttons are used, and they work whether or not you accept cookies.

The only non-essential cookies on this Site are those of Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited), used solely to measure advertising conversions, that is, whether a visit that came from one of our ads ends in a form submission or a tap on the WhatsApp button. They are set only if you click “Accept” in the cookie banner: until then the Google tag is not loaded. The usual ones are _gcl_au and, when you arrive from an ad, _gcl_aw, which last up to 90 days. Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the banner is not shown again, and you can change it at any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw your consent, we delete those cookies.

The embedded Cal.com scheduler may set strictly technical cookies or receive your IP address when it loads. Fonts are served from this Site’s own domain. All of these services are governed by their own privacy policies.

When you submit a form on this Site (diagnosis, calculator, professional request or payment), your browser keeps a temporary copy of what you entered in its local storage (localStorage) only until the submission is completed, so it is not lost if the connection fails; it is deleted as soon as the submission succeeds. This copy is not a cookie, never leaves your device on its own and is not used to track you; you can also remove it at any time by clearing this Site’s data in your browser.

11. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures (art. 32 GDPR) to protect personal data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls and data minimisation, in particular, the Site’s interactive tools are designed to run locally in your browser. The documents you upload to a case file are encrypted by us (AES-GCM) before they are stored, on top of the storage provider’s own encryption at rest, and they are only reachable through links that expire; the links shared with a professional can also be revoked.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal or operational changes. The date of the current version is shown at the top of this page; material changes will be highlighted on the Site.
