Frequently asked questions

How strict is the 6-month deadline? ＋

Absolute. Modelo 149 must be filed within 6 months of your Spanish Social Security registration. There are no extensions and no exceptions — missing it means losing the regime permanently.

Are the Digital Nomad Visa and the Beckham Law the same thing? ＋

No. The visa (UGE) is an immigration procedure; the Beckham regime (AEAT, Modelo 149) is a separate tax election with its own deadline. You can hold the visa and still lose the tax regime by filing late.

Can my spouse and children join the regime? ＋

Yes — the regime extends to your spouse and children under 25 under certain conditions. We handle the family filing as an add-on (+40%).

I am a freelancer. Do I qualify? ＋

Pure freelancing invoicing clients does not qualify by default. Structuring routes exist (qualified professional / entrepreneur). We assess viability in a paid consultation before you spend anything else.

What about Wealth Tax and Modelo 720? ＋

Under the regime you are taxed as a non-resident: foreign capital income stays outside Spanish income tax, no Modelo 720, and Wealth Tax applies only to Spanish assets.

What happens after year 6? ＋

The regime lasts 6 tax years. We build your exit strategy in years 5–6: transition to standard income tax or an exit plan, so the end of the regime never catches you off guard.
