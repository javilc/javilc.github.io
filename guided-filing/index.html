Is this for you?

Only for cases that meet ALL of these points:

  • ✓You have not been tax resident in Spain in any of the 5 previous years.
  • ✓Your situation is one of these three: an employment contract with a Spanish company; remote work for a foreign employer with no office, subsidiary or branch in Spain; or a posting to Spain by your employer.
  • ✓You are clear about your Spanish Social Security registration or your certificate of coverage (A1 or bilateral agreement).
  • ✓You are not a shareholder or director of the company that pays you, or of any company in Spain.
  • ✓You are not self-employed and do not invoice clients.
  • ✓You have no stock options, RSUs or significant bonuses pending.
  • ✓You are not a US citizen paid through your own LLC or S-corp.

Questions

How can it cost so much less than a firm? ＋

Because you file it yourself, and only simple cases that meet every criterion above qualify. A firm’s fee covers representation before the tax agency, liability for the filing and complex cases; Guided filing gives you the preparation, the deadline and the guide, and leaves the filing in your hands.

What if my situation changes halfway through? ＋

Run the diagnosis again before you file: it is what decides whether your case still meets the criteria. If it no longer does, the result sends you to a registered professional, who quotes their own fee, and the fit guarantee applies: write to us within 30 days of buying and we refund you in full or put the price towards that fee. Your case file reflects what you tell it, so update it when something changes.

Does it include the US side? ＋

No. It covers the Spanish Modelo 149 and the annual reminders. IRS obligations (FEIE or Foreign Tax Credit, FBAR, FATCA) are not included.

What happens if I make a mistake when filing? ＋

The mandatory-field check before you sign tells you if a required box is empty or a document is missing. Every value in the guide comes from your case file, where you confirmed it, and you review the form on the tax agency’s site before signing it with your own credentials.
